Two ways to secure rake capacity -- and how blending them changes cost, service and risk.

Base Fleet captive / leased, GPWIS & SFTO

Advantages
  • ●Higher availability certainty
  • ●Lower marginal dependence on spot IR allotments
  • ●Better protection during demand spikes
Disadvantages
  • ●Fixed commitment regardless of daily demand
  • ●Underutilization risk on low-demand days

Flexible / Spot Fleet FOIS spot bookings

Advantages
  • ●Flexible, booked only when needed
  • ●Lower fixed commitment
  • ●Useful for absorbing demand spikes
Disadvantages
  • ●Availability uncertainty (rake allotment not guaranteed)
  • ●Variable, less predictable cost
  • ●Potential demurrage / shortage risk

Base Fleet % vs Spot Fleet % strategy spectrum

Service Level, Spot Dependency and Risk are natural percentages; Total Cost is indexed 0–100 (lowest–highest observed on this spectrum) so all four can share one axis. The optimization engine (Screen 07) finds the actual cost-minimizing point on this curve.
