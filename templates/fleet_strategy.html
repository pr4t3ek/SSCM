Contract Clauses to Negotiate

  • ●Volume flexibility band (±15%) on the core commitment — absorbs forecast error without reopening the contract.
  • ●Liquidated damages for non-supply by the lessor — makes wagon availability the counterparty's risk rather than CIL's. The commercial twin of the Tier 3 option.
  • ●Seasonal rate differentiation — a lower committed rate in lean months, traded for a firm peak-season commitment.
  • ●Two-tier demurrage (free time, then a penal slab) — already priced in the cost model; see Appendix A4.
  • ●Backhaul / return-load sharing in the lean season — monetises rakes that would otherwise sit idle.
