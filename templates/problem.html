In short

  • ●Relying solely on spot Indian Railways rake allotments exposes CIL to wagon shortages during demand surges.
  • ●The same spot-only reliance creates demurrage / overbooking exposure during demand slumps.
  • ●A mixed captive/leased (GPWIS/SFTO) base fleet plus spot bookings can absorb this volatility at lower expected cost -- the question this dashboard answers.
