How to Contract It

The cost model gives a base-vs-spot ratio. This is the procurement structure that delivers it — four tiers sized to the observed seasonality, so lean-month capacity is not paid for at peak-month levels. Detail and the seasonality behind it are on Screen 06.

  • ●Tier 3 is the answer to wagon availability. A reservation fee buys the right to a rake at a pre-agreed strike, converting "the market may not have one" from an operational risk into a contractual guarantee.
  • ●Pair it with liquidated damages for non-supply so the lessor carries the availability risk, and a ±15% volume flexibility band so forecast error does not reopen the contract.
  • ●Negotiate seasonal rate differentiation: a lower committed rate through the lean months in exchange for a firm peak-season commitment.
