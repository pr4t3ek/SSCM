The consulting-style close: what we found, what we built, and what CIL should do next.

What We Observed

Demand and dispatch exhibit significant shift-level variability, creating persistent periods of unmet demand at Lakhanpur OCP.

What We Optimized

Fleet mix (Base vs. Spot rakes) was optimized against expected total cost, service level and procurement risk, using an exact convex-optimization engine over the observed daily requirement.

What We Recommend

Adopt the model-recommended Base + Flexible fleet structure, with periodic recalibration as demand and railway availability change.

Management Action Plan

  1. 1Secure Base Fleet capacity for predictable, recurring demand.
  2. 2Retain Spot Fleet bookings as a peak-demand buffer.
  3. 3Monitor demand and dispatch weekly against the dashboard's KPIs.
  4. 4Re-optimize the contracting mix whenever demand or railway availability changes materially.
